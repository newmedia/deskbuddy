deskbuddy.dev

This app talks to your deskbuddy over Bluetooth. Tap Connect via Bluetooth; the first time, your phone asks for the pairing code that appears on the deskbuddy's screen.

Emotion

Actions

Sound & screen

It reacts to clapping, loud noise and music (then it dances along). Only the sound level is measured; nothing is recorded or sent.

Setting up a new board, or picking a specific version (including an older one)? You can do that from the browser with a USB cable, without building anything.
