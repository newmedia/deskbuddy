deskbuddy.dev

This app talks to your deskbuddy over Bluetooth. Tap Connect via Bluetooth; the first time, your phone asks for the pairing code that appears on the deskbuddy's screen.

Emotion

Actions

Sound & screen

It reacts to clapping, loud noise and music (then it dances along). Only the sound level is measured; nothing is recorded or sent.

Buddies nearby

Buddies in the same group see each other (in the same room, also without a router) and catch each other's yawns.

Not in a group yet.

To join, ask someone in the group to show the invite on their buddy (Buddies tab), and scan it with the camera of the phone that controls your buddy. Or paste the invite below.
